
# Smart Farmer Agricultural Knowledge Sources

## 1. Rwanda Soil Information System (RwaSIS)
Organization: Rwanda Agriculture and Animal Resources Development Board
Website: https://rwasis.rab.gov.rw/
Purpose:
- Parcel-specific soil information
- Soil composition
- Crop suitability information
- Soil fertility and erosion information

Priority: High

## Knowledge Base Rules

1. Prefer official Rwanda agricultural sources.
2. Record the source and publication date of every document.
3. Never treat estimated soil information as verified.
4. Do not invent crop requirements.
5. Keep general agricultural guidance separate from Rwanda-validated evidence.
6. Every AI-generated agricultural recommendation must be traceable to supporting evidence.
